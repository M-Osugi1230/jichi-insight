# Kanagawa Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Kanagawa cluster stage 1 (Issue #790) — entity identity, the
current comprehensive plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements and the governor's
current term.

- Municipality: 神奈川県 (`jp-local-140007`, official code 140007). The
  code is confirmed by the corporate number 1000020140007 printed on the
  prefecture's official pages (a check digit followed by 000020 and the
  code) and by the MIC settlement cards.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 2,375,969,000 thousand yen and
  prefectural tax revenue 1,525,483,133 thousand yen, from Bill No. 1 of
  the prefecture (定県第1号議案）, submitted on 2026-02-12 and passed as
  proposed on 2026-03-25 at the 1st regular session of 2026.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were cross-checked
  between two primary sources — the MIC settlement cards （都道府県決算
  カード, Kanagawa sheet) and the prefecture's own fiscal situation
  collections （財政状況資料集）. All values agreed. Arithmetic checks
  (revenue − expenditure = formal balance; formal balance − carryover
  resources = real balance; purpose/nature breakdown sums = total
  expenditure; the three fund categories sum to the fund balance) all
  pass. Ratios are registered as published, never recomputed; the
  labelled current-balance ratio is used rather than the parenthesised
  variant. Categories shown as "－" in the sources are omitted from the
  breakdowns (no value is guessed or zero-filled).
- Plan: 新かながわグランドデザイン (adopted March 2024) — a basic concept
  looking ahead to 2040 plus an implementation plan for FY2024-FY2027
  with five themes and 13 projects; progress is governed by annual
  evaluation reports with four-stage project judgements by the
  Comprehensive Plan Council. No project-level policy-evaluation sheet
  exists, so projects and their evaluations are registered in the
  project stage.
- Governor: 黒岩祐治， fourth term, 2023-04-23 to 2027-04-22. The term
  end is stated day-exact in the prefectural election commission's
  term-expiry list; the start follows the Article 259 proviso of the
  Public Offices Election Act (term-expiry election held before the
  previous term expired) and agrees with the stated end.
