# Iwate Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Iwate cluster stage 1 (Issue #1070) — entity identity,
the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, and the
governor's current term. Iwate Prefecture had no earlier
reviewed registration in this repository (no reviewed package,
no fiscal or settlement records), so this package is a new
registration standing on the prefecture's and the MIC's own
published documents alone; nothing was replaced.

- Municipality: 岩手県 (`jp-local-030007`, official code 030007).
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 774,233,557
  thousand yen and prefectural tax revenue 135,788,000
  thousand yen, from Bill No. 1 of the February 2026 regular
  session (議案第1号「令和8年度岩手県一般会計予算」), passed on
  2026-03-23 with the result stated verbatim as
  「意見を付し原案可決」 in the assembly's bill list (the
  member-by-member vote table prints 「意見を付して原案可決」).
  The figures are the printed values in the summary tables of
  the prefecture's budget explanation document
  (『令和8年度予算に関する説明書』, itemised statement of the
  general-account budget); the revenue-category components
  sum to the total. The total also equals the pre-revision
  amount in Table 1 of the FY2026 general-account
  supplementary budget (No. 1), and the tax figure is outside
  the scope of supplementary budget (No. 2), whose itemised
  statement leaves it at the initial value.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between the MIC settlement cards
  (都道府県決算カード, Iwate sheet of the all-prefecture
  workbook) and the fiscal situation collections
  (財政状況資料集) for Iwate published on the MIC site. All
  values agreed on every indicator and every breakdown
  category in all three years; nothing was guessed or
  recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure =
  formal balance; formal balance − carryover resources =
  real balance; purpose/nature breakdown sums = total
  expenditure; the fund categories sum to the fund balance)
  all pass. Ratios are registered as published, never
  recomputed; the labelled current-balance ratio is used
  rather than the parenthesised variant. Cross-check design
  limit: both systems follow the national
  settlement-statistics formats and rest on the same
  underlying statistics, so the cross-check detects
  transcription and aggregation errors but is not an
  independent verification of the statistics themselves;
  the prefecture's statutory settlement (決算の要領告示) is
  on a general/special-account basis and cannot
  independently reproduce the ordinary-account aggregates.
  This limit is recorded in every settlement evidence
  packet. The printed "地方債現在高（臨時財政対策債を除く）"
  of the collections is registered as a breakdown item and
  the temporary fiscal measures bond balance is the derived
  difference, marked as such in the breakdown note.
- Governor: 達増 拓也, fifth term, 2023-09-11 to 2027-09-10.
  He won the 2023-09-03 term-expiry election with 336,502
  votes — the prefectural total in the election commission's
  ballot-count form (the two candidates' votes sum to the
  valid-vote total of 568,617); the winner was announced in
  the prefectural gazette (election commission notice
  No. 82, 2023-09-06), and the federation of prefectural
  election commissions' governor list prints the same vote
  count and the term-end date 2027-09-10. No press figure is
  used. The start date 2023-09-11 is printed directly in the
  governor's official profile (which lists every term), and
  the end date 2027-09-10 is printed directly in the
  federation's list, so both ends of the term rest on
  printed official values. The 2023 election bulletin no
  longer appears on the election commission's current
  official pages (stage-0 open question OQ-1); a copy
  survives on an election-information site's CDN and was
  collated for content (title, issuing commission, both
  candidate columns, and the candidate profile, which
  agrees with the governor's official profile), but its
  digest could not be collated against an official
  original. The bulletin is registered as a manifesto
  source with that limit recorded; promise extraction is
  deferred to stage 3.
- Review delegation: the review and promotion judgements in
  this package were made by Muse under the repository
  owner's delegation of 2026-11-15; they are not recorded as
  the owner's own review.
