# Okayama Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Okayama cluster stage 1 (Issue #954) — entity identity,
the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, and the
governor's current term. Okayama Prefecture had no earlier
reviewed registration in this repository, so this package
stands on the prefecture's and the MIC's own published
documents alone.

- Municipality: 岡山県 (`jp-local-330001`, official code 330001).
  The prefecture's corporate number 4000020330001 (stated in
  the footer of the official prefecture site) ends in the
  code, and the MIC settlement cards carry the Okayama sheet
  under code 33.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 819,698,359
  thousand yen and prefectural tax revenue 280,362,922
  thousand yen, from the prefectural gazette Extra of
  2026-03-19 (Prefectural Notice No. 129, Article 1 and
  Table 1). Bill No. 1 of the February 2026 regular session
  (議第1号「令和8年度岡山県一般会計予算」) was passed on
  2026-03-19 with the result stated verbatim as 「原案可決」
  in the assembly's published bill list. The stage-0
  inventory could only confirm rounded hundred-million-yen
  figures; the gazette resolves both amounts to the exact
  thousand-yen printed values. The prefecture's publicity
  materials round the total to the hundred-million-yen
  level, which is not used.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between two primary sources — the MIC
  settlement cards (都道府県決算カード, Okayama sheet) and the
  fiscal situation collections (財政状況資料集) compiled by
  the prefecture and hosted on the MIC site. The collection
  was chosen as the second system because the prefecture's
  own settlement explanatory booklet is published by account
  (general and special accounts) and carries no
  ordinary-account indicators, so the collection is the most
  verifiable prefecture-side publication for ordinary-account
  values. All values agreed on every indicator and every
  breakdown category in all three years; nothing was guessed
  or recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure =
  formal balance; formal balance − carryover resources =
  real balance; purpose/nature breakdown sums = total
  expenditure; the fund categories sum to the fund balance)
  all pass. Ratios are registered as published, never
  recomputed; the labelled current-balance ratio is used
  rather than the parenthesised variant (97.7 / 97.5 /
  94.7). Two printed quirks are kept as printed: the
  purpose-table disaster recovery amount differs slightly
  from the nature-table one in both sources (by 5, 2 and 1
  thousand yen respectively), and the purpose and nature
  tables classify 公債費 differently. Unlike Kyoto, the
  debt-reduction fund （減債基金） has a balance in all three
  years, so the fund balance consists of three categories.
  The collections' printed "地方債現在高（臨時財政対策債を除く）"
  is registered as a breakdown item and the temporary fiscal
  measures bond balance is the derived difference, marked as
  such in the breakdown note.
- Governor: 伊原木 隆太, fourth term, 2024-11-12 to
  2028-11-11. He won the 2024-10-27 term-expiry election
  with 634,587 votes (prefectural total in the election
  commission's own ballot-count results; the 634,586 figure
  in Wikipedia's list of governors is wrong). The expiry
  date is stated directly in the election commission's
  official term list (as of 2026-05-26); the start follows
  the Article 259 proviso construction from the previous
  term's expiry date 2024-11-11, which the commission's
  election overview states directly. No official document
  directly printing the inauguration date was confirmed in
  this stage, and the 2024 election bulletin is not
  currently linked from the commission's governor-election
  page, so the term's manifesto sources stay empty until
  stage 3 re-checks both points.
