# Kumamoto Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Kumamoto cluster stage 1 (Issue #956) — entity identity,
the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, and the
governor's current term. Kumamoto Prefecture had no earlier
reviewed registration in this repository, so this package stands
on the prefecture's and the MIC's own published documents alone.

- Municipality: 熊本県 (`jp-local-430005`, official code 430005).
  The prefecture's corporate number 7000020430005 (stated in
  the footer of the official prefecture site) ends in the
  code, and the MIC settlement cards carry the Kumamoto sheet
  under code 44.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 935,335,562
  thousand yen and prefectural tax revenue 177,985,266
  thousand yen, from Bill No. 46 of the February 2026 regular
  session (議案第46号「令和8年度熊本県一般会計予算」), passed on
  2026-03-18 with the result stated verbatim as 「可決」. The
  bill PDF is image-only, so the thousand-yen figures are a
  visual transcription of its Table 1; the tax total also
  equals the sum of its eleven itemised components. The same
  pre-revision total appears in Table 1 of the June 2026
  supplementary budget bill (No. 1), whose Article 1 states a
  post-revision total of 953,590,591 thousand yen and a
  revision amount of 18,255,029 thousand yen. The budget
  outline rounds the figures to the million-yen level
  (935,336 million yen / 177,985 million yen), which is not
  used.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between two MIC publications — the MIC
  settlement cards (都道府県決算カード, Kumamoto sheet of the
  all-prefecture workbook) and the fiscal situation
  collections (財政状況資料集) hosted on the MIC site. All
  values agreed on every indicator and every breakdown
  category in all three years; nothing was guessed or
  recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure =
  formal balance; formal balance − carryover resources = real
  balance; purpose/nature breakdown sums = total expenditure;
  the fund categories sum to the fund balance) all pass.
  Ratios are registered as published, never recomputed; the
  labelled current-balance ratio is used rather than the
  parenthesised variant (90.5 / 91.5 / 91.5). Cross-check
  design limit: the prefecture's own fiscal-situation page
  states that it republishes the MIC data as-is, so it cannot
  serve as an independent second system, and the prefecture's
  statutory settlement documents (決算書・決算の概要) are on a
  general/special-account basis and cannot independently
  reproduce the ordinary-account aggregates; the cross-check
  is therefore between the two MIC publications, and this
  limit is recorded in every settlement evidence packet.
  Unlike Kyoto, the debt-reduction fund （減債基金） carries a
  balance in all three years, so the fund balance consists of
  three categories. The printed "地方債現在高（臨時財政対策債
  除き）" of the collections is registered as a breakdown item
  and the temporary fiscal measures bond balance is the
  derived difference, marked as such in the breakdown note.
- Governor: 木村 敬, first term, 2024-04-16 to 2028-04-15.
  He won the 2024-03-24 term-expiry election with 383,010
  votes (prefectural total in the election commission's own
  ballot-count results; turnout 49.63%). The expiry date is
  stated directly in the election commission's official term
  list (市町村長・議員の任期満了日等に関する調べ, as of
  2026-08-01), whose PDF was retrieved and read directly in
  this stage. The start date is stated by the governor
  himself in the official record of his 2024-04-16
  inauguration press conference ("本日、令和6年4月16日付で
  熊本県知事に就任いたしました") and agrees with the Article
  259 proviso construction from the previous term's expiry
  (2024-04-15). The election proclamation date has not yet
  been confirmed in the prefectural gazette and is left as an
  open question in the evidence packet.
