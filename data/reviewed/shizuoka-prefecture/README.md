# Shizuoka Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Shizuoka cluster stages 1-2 (Issues #891, #896) — entity
identity, the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account
initial budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements,
the governor's current term, and ten priority-project series
registered at the policy-pillar level of the plan evaluation
report. This package is separate from the Phase 9 registration for
Shizuoka Prefecture (`data/reviewed/phase9/22.json`, 985 reviewed
target statements extracted from the same comprehensive plan), which
carries no fiscal or executive records; the two do not conflict.

- Municipality: 静岡県 (`jp-local-220001`, official code 220001). The
  prefecture's corporate number 7000020220001 (stated on the official
  prefecture site) ends in the code, and the MIC settlement cards
  carry the Shizuoka sheet under code 22.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 1,414,100,000 thousand yen
  and prefectural tax revenue 542,000,000 thousand yen, from Bill
  No. 1 of the February 2026 regular session
  (第1号議案「令和8年度静岡県一般会計予算」), passed unanimously on
  2026-03-16 (「可決・全員一致」, no amendments). The prefecture's
  published summaries state the budget only in millions of yen
  (1,414,100 million yen; tax 542,000 million yen), so the
  thousand-yen figures were confirmed in Bill No. 96 (the FY2026
  general-account supplementary budget), whose Table 1 states the
  pre-supplement amounts — the enacted initial budget itself — in
  thousand yen. The two agree exactly.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between two primary sources — the MIC settlement
  cards (都道府県決算カード, Shizuoka sheet) and the fiscal situation
  collections (財政状況資料集) as hosted on the MIC site. All values
  agreed on every indicator in all three years; nothing was guessed
  or recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure = formal
  balance; formal balance − carryover resources = real balance;
  purpose/nature breakdown sums = total expenditure; the fund
  categories sum to the fund balance) all pass. Ratios are
  registered as published, never recomputed; the labelled
  current-balance ratio is used rather than the parenthesised
  variant. Categories shown as "－" in the sources are omitted from
  the breakdowns (no value is guessed or zero-filled): 消防費・
  諸支出金・前年度繰上充用金・特別区財政調整交付金 in the purpose
  breakdown and 前年度繰上充用金・失業対策事業費 in the nature
  breakdown, in every year. The automobile acquisition tax grant in
  FY2024 is printed as 4 thousand yen and is registered as printed.
  All three fund categories carry published balances in all three
  years.
- Plan: the top plan is the Shizuoka Prefecture Comprehensive Plan
  "Well-being Plan" (「静岡県総合計画～しずおか ウェルビーイング
  プラン～」, FY2025-FY2028), whose stated aim is 「幸福度日本一の
  静岡県」. Its structure is three policy divisions
  (「未来を創る力」「豊かな暮らし」「県民の安心」) plus
  administrative management, above eight policies, twenty-four
  policy pillars, 197 initiatives and 101 outcome indicators.
  Progress is governed by the annual plan evaluation report
  (静岡県総合計画評価書), which grades the outcome indicators A-D
  by progress rate; the FY2026 report (evaluating the plan's first
  year, FY2025) was published on 2026-10-30. This review is
  consistent with the Phase 9 registration of 985 reviewed target
  statements from the same plan.
- Priority projects (stage 2, Issue #896): the registration unit is
  the policy pillar (政策の柱, the twenty-four middle pillars),
  because the annual plan evaluation report publishes, for each
  pillar, its outcome indicators with baseline, current and target
  values and an A-D grade, plus its main initiatives; the eight
  policies are recorded as each series' parent policy. Ten series
  were selected by cycling through the eight policies in the plan's
  published order and taking each policy's pillars in published
  order (round 1: the first pillar of each policy; round 2: the
  second pillars of Industry and of Environment/Energy). All 46
  outcome indicators of the ten pillars are registered as published
  in the FY2026 evaluation report (the evaluation of FY2025, the
  plan's first year, published October 2026): values are registered
  exactly as printed, including indicators whose current value is
  an earlier year's figure and indicators whose current value is
  not yet published (registered with no value and the scheduled
  publication noted). The prefecture publishes no per-pillar
  rating, so official_evaluation stays unregistered; the official
  grades attach to the indicators (A-D by progress rate or by
  comparison with the target, "－" when no actual has been
  published) and are converted to the registration assessment scale
  on each KPI. The prefecture also publishes no per-pillar budget
  or settlement amount, so amounts stay unregistered.
- Governor: 鈴木 康友, current term 2024-05-26 to 2028-05-25. He
  won the vacancy election of 2024-05-26 — held after Governor
  Kawakatsu Heita resigned; all six candidates were newcomers —
  with 728,500 votes (47.42%, turnout 52.47%). Because this was a
  vacancy election, the term runs from the election day under the
  main text of Article 259 of the Public Offices Election Act (the
  election certificate was issued and he took office on
  2024-05-28). The expiry date 2028-05-25 is also stated verbatim
  on the prefectural election commission's official term page
  (「市町長及び議会議員の任期」), so the statutory construction and
  the commission's own statement agree. The vote total was checked
  directly against the Minamiizu Town election commission's
  official ballot-count result, which states the prefecture-wide
  total, and against the Shizuoka City election commission's
  election results report; the prefectural commission's own
  ballot-count document has not been located on its current site
  (its turnout report survives), which is recorded as an open
  question in the evidence packet. Earlier terms are not
  registered.
