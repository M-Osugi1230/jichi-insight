# Saitama Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Saitama cluster stage 1 (Issue #815 (1)) — entity identity, the
current comprehensive plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements and the governor's
current term.

- Municipality: 埼玉県 (`jp-local-110001`, official code 110001), as
  carried by the MIC settlement cards and the prefecture's fiscal
  situation collections.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 2,434,865,000 thousand yen and
  prefectural tax revenue 905,200,000 thousand yen, from Bill No. 1 of
  the prefecture （第1号議案「令和8年度埼玉県一般会計予算」), submitted to
  the February 2026 regular session (convened on 2026-02-19) and passed
  as proposed on 2026-03-27 （原案可決； the JCP and ネット voted against).
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were cross-checked
  between two primary sources — the MIC settlement cards （都道府県決算
  カード, Saitama sheet) and the prefecture's own fiscal situation
  collections （財政状況資料集）. All values agreed; for FY2024 the
  prefecture-published file and the MIC-hosted copy of the collection
  also agree on every value. Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure =
  formal balance; formal balance − carryover resources = real balance;
  purpose/nature breakdown sums = total expenditure; the three fund
  categories sum to the fund balance) all pass. Ratios are registered as
  published, never recomputed; the labelled current-balance ratio is
  used rather than the parenthesised variant. Categories shown as "－"
  in the sources are omitted from the breakdowns (no value is guessed
  or zero-filled).
- Plan: 埼玉県5か年計画 ～日本一暮らしやすい埼玉へ～ (FY2022-FY2026) —
  three visions looking ahead to 2040, twelve courses and 54 field-level
  measures, each with policy indicators; a rolling mid-term revision was
  carried out in March 2025. Progress is governed by annual policy
  evaluations of the 54 measures (indicator progress sheets, records of
  main initiatives and a residents' satisfaction survey). The next plan
  (FY2027-FY2031) is still being drafted, so its measures are not
  registered and no cross-version mapping is made.
- Governor: 大野元裕, second term, 2023-08-31 to 2027-08-30. Both the
  start and the end are stated day-exact in the Saitama City election
  commission's official ballot-count table （当選人の任期 令和5年8月31日
  ～令和9年8月30日）; the end is also stated in the prefectural election
  commission's term-expiry list. His 1,138,973 votes are confirmed in
  both election commissions' publications.

- The 54 field-level measures of the five-year plan and their
  evaluations
- Assembly records
- FY2026 execution and settlement figures
