# Chiba Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Chiba cluster stage 1 (Issue #835 (1)) — entity identity, the
current comprehensive plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements and the
governor's current term.

- Municipality: 千葉県 (`jp-local-120006`, official code 120006), as
  carried by the MIC settlement cards and the fiscal situation
  collections. The prefecture's official pages print the corporate
  number 4000020120006, whose last six digits match the code.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 2,253,487,205 thousand yen
  and prefectural tax revenue 1,028,324,000 thousand yen, from Bill
  No. 1 of the prefecture (議案第1号「令和8年度千葉県一般会計予算」),
  submitted to the February 2026 regular session on 2026-02-10 and
  passed as proposed on 2026-03-13 (原案可決), as recorded in the
  assembly's official bill list.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between two primary sources — the MIC settlement
  cards (都道府県決算カード, Chiba sheet) and the fiscal situation
  collections (財政状況資料集) as hosted on the MIC site. All values
  agreed on every indicator, including the purpose and nature
  breakdowns. The prefecture's own fiscal pages carry only the
  FY2024 edition of the collection, so the MIC-hosted copies were
  used for FY2022 and FY2023 (they are the prefecture-produced
  files). Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure = formal balance;
  formal balance − carryover resources = real balance; purpose/nature
  breakdown sums = total expenditure; the three fund categories sum
  to the fund balance) all pass. Ratios are registered as published,
  never recomputed; the labelled current-balance ratio is used
  rather than the parenthesised variant. Categories shown as "－"
  in the sources are omitted from the breakdowns (no value is
  guessed or zero-filled): 消防費・諸支出金・前年度繰上充用金・
  特別区財政調整交付金・特別地方消費税交付金 in every year, and
  自動車取得税交付金 in FY2024 (it has values in FY2022-FY2023).
- Plan: 千葉県総合計画 ～千葉の未来をともに創る～ (decided
  2025-10-10 after the September 2025 regular session's vote) — a
  basic-concept part looking roughly ten years ahead and an
  implementation-plan part for FY2025-FY2028, structured as six
  basic goals, fifteen policies, forty-one measures and two hundred
  main initiatives, with 89 social-goal indicators, 40 supporting
  indicators and 455 administrative-activity indicators. Progress is
  governed by annual policy evaluations of the previous year's
  implementation, compiled with the opinions of external experts;
  the first evaluation (FY2025 implementation) found 56 of the 89
  social-goal indicators progressed from their baselines, 34 of
  them at or above the benchmark level. The previous plan (～新しい
  千葉の時代を切り開く～, implementation period FY2022-FY2024) has a
  different structure, so its measures and indicators are not mapped
  across versions.
- Governor: 熊谷俊人, second term, 2025-04-05 to 2029-04-04. The
  prefectural election commission states that the 2025 election was
  held because the previous term expired on 2025-04-04, so the start
  follows Article 259's proviso (the day after the previous term's
  expiry); the end (2029-04-04) is stated day-exact in the Kashiwa
  City election commission's official term-expiry list and agrees
  with the statutory four-year term. His 1,404,905 votes are the
  prefectural election commission's official figure (Wikipedia's
  1,405,905 is a transcription variant and is not used).
