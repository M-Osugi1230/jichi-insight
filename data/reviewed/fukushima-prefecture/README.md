# Fukushima Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Fukushima cluster stage 1 (Issue #1012) — entity identity,
the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, and the
governor's current term. Fukushima Prefecture had no earlier
reviewed registration in this repository (no reviewed package,
no fiscal or settlement records), so this package is a new
registration standing on the prefecture's and the MIC's own
published documents alone; nothing was replaced.

- Municipality: 福島県 (`jp-local-070009`, official code 070009).
  The prefecture's corporate number 7000020070009 (National Tax
  Agency corporate-number publication site) ends in the code.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 1,260,607,000
  thousand yen and prefectural tax revenue 241,794,000
  thousand yen, from Bill No. 1 of the February 2026 regular
  session (議案第1号「令和8年度福島県一般会計予算」), passed on
  2026-03-19 with the result stated verbatim as 「原案可決」.
  The figures are the printed values in Table 1 of the
  prefectural gazette extra issue 『福島県の財政』 No. 157
  (FY2026 initial budget); the revenue-category components sum
  to the total. The tax figure also equals the
  pre-revision amount in Table 1 of the FY2026 general-account
  supplementary budget (No. 2) itemised statement.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between the MIC settlement cards
  (都道府県決算カード, Fukushima sheet of the all-prefecture
  workbook) and the fiscal situation collections
  (財政状況資料集) published by the prefecture itself on its
  official site. All values agreed on every indicator and
  every breakdown category in all three years; nothing was
  guessed or recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue −
  expenditure = formal balance; formal balance − carryover
  resources = real balance; purpose/nature breakdown sums =
  total expenditure; the fund categories sum to the fund
  balance) all pass. Ratios are registered as published, never
  recomputed; the labelled current-balance ratio is used
  rather than the parenthesised variant. Cross-check design
  limit: both systems follow the national settlement-statistics
  formats and rest on the same underlying statistics, so the
  cross-check detects transcription and aggregation errors but
  is not an independent verification of the statistics
  themselves; the prefecture's statutory settlement documents
  (決算書・決算の概要) are on a general/special-account basis
  and cannot independently reproduce the ordinary-account
  aggregates. This limit is recorded in every settlement
  evidence packet. The printed "地方債現在高（臨時財政対策債
  除き）" of the collections is registered as a breakdown item
  and the temporary fiscal measures bond balance is the
  derived difference, marked as such in the breakdown note.
- Governor: 内堀 雅雄, fourth term, 2026-11-12 to 2030-11-11.
  He won the 2026-10-25 term-expiry election with 585,641
  votes — the prefectural total in the final ballot-count
  file (確定開票) published on the election commission's
  official results site; the seven candidates' votes sum to
  the valid-vote total of 645,250, and turnout was 44.05%
  (final turnout file: 1,477,389 electors, 650,859 voters).
  No press figure is used. The previous term's expiry date
  (2026-11-11) is stated directly in the election
  commission's official election-schedule publication, so the
  start date follows from the proviso of Article 259 of the
  Public Offices Election Act and agrees with the governor's
  official first-attendance record on 2026-11-12 (「知事初登庁
  （4期目）」in 知事の動き). The end date 2030-11-11 is the
  four-year term under Article 140 of the Local Autonomy Act
  constructed from that start; no commission document printing
  the end date itself has been located yet, and this is kept
  as an open question in the evidence packet. The 2026
  election bulletin survives on the election commission's
  current official pages and is registered as a manifesto
  source; promise extraction is deferred to stage 3.
- Review delegation: the review and promotion judgements in
  this package were made by Muse under the repository
  owner's delegation of 2026-11-15; they are not recorded as
  the owner's own review.
