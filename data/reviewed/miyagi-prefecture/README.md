# Miyagi Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Miyagi cluster stages 1-2 (Issues #874 and #878) — entity
identity, the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, the governor's
current term, and ten priority-project series registered at the
measure level of the prefecture's policy-evaluation system.

- Municipality: 宮城県 (`jp-local-040002`, official code 040002), as
  carried by the MIC settlement cards. The prefecture's corporate
  number 8000020040002 (National Tax Agency corporate-number site)
  ends in the code.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 1,095,923,909 thousand yen
  and prefectural tax revenue 344,600,000 thousand yen, from Bill
  No. 1 of the 399th regular session (第1号議案「令和8年度宮城県
  一般会計予算」), passed as proposed on 2026-03-18 (原案可決), as
  published in the prefecture's fiscal-status report No. 152
  (「財政状況」第152号, June 2026, a supplement to the Miyagi
  Prefecture official gazette).
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between two primary sources — the MIC settlement
  cards (都道府県決算カード, Miyagi sheet) and the fiscal situation
  collections (財政状況資料集) as hosted on the MIC site. All values
  agreed on every indicator in all three years; nothing was guessed
  or recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure = formal
  balance; formal balance − carryover resources = real balance;
  purpose/nature breakdown sums = total expenditure; the fund
  categories sum to the fund balance) all pass. Ratios are
  registered as published, never recomputed; the labelled
  current-balance ratio is used rather than the parenthesised
  variant. Categories shown as "－" in the sources are omitted from
  the breakdowns (no value is guessed or zero-filled): 消防費・
  諸支出金・前年度繰上充用金・特別区財政調整交付金 in every year,
  and 自動車取得税交付金 in FY2024 (it has values in FY2022-FY2023).
  The debt reduction fund (減債基金) has a published balance in all
  three years, so all three fund categories carry values.
- Plan: the top plan is the ten-year Shin-Miyagi Future Vision
  (「新・宮城の将来ビジョン」, FY2021-FY2030, adopted under the
  prefectural slogan 「富県躍進！」), which is also the prefectural
  comprehensive strategy under Article 9 of the Act on Overcoming
  Population Decline and Vitalizing Local Economy in Local
  Communities. Its structure is "one plus four" pillars — the
  recovery pillar 「被災地の復興完了に向けたきめ細かなサポート」
  (four areas) plus four basic directions — above eight policies
  (the eight 「つくる」) and eighteen measures. Implementation runs
  through the implementation plan in three periods (first
  FY2021-2024, midterm FY2025-2027, final FY2028-2030); the midterm
  plan (revised February 2026) is current, and progress is governed
  by the prefecture's policy-evaluation system, which publishes
  policy and measure evaluations annually. This review is consistent
  with the Phase 9-11 registrations (the policy review manifest and
  the verified KPI index of 128 target groups and 149 indicator
  series).
- Governor: 村井 嘉浩, current term 2025-11-21 to 2029-11-20. He
  was re-elected for a sixth term — the most in the prefecture's
  history — in the term-expiry election of 2025-10-26 with 340,190
  votes, as stated in the Sendai City election commission's official
  ballot-count result (the Miyagi-wide total); the prefectural
  election commission's results summary stated the same figure (its
  special results site has since closed). Because this was a
  term-expiry election, the term starts the day after the previous
  term expired (2025-11-20, as stated on the prefectural election
  commission's official election page) under the proviso of Article
  259 of the Public Offices Election Act. Earlier terms (2005-2025)
  are not registered, following the treatment of predecessor terms
  in earlier clusters. The cross-check of the 2029-11-20 expiry
  against a statement in the prefectural election commission's own
  publications is still outstanding (its term-expiry list covers
  municipal mayors and assembly members, not the governor) and is
  recorded as an open question in the evidence packet.

- Priority projects (stage 2, Issue #878): the registration unit is
  the measure (施策, the eighteen 取組), because the prefecture's
  policy-evaluation system publishes a measure evaluation for each
  of the eighteen measures every year, with the measure's rating and
  the achievement status of its target indicators; the eight
  policies are recorded as each series' parent policy. Ten series
  were selected by cycling through the eight policies in the
  vision's published order and taking each policy's measures in
  published order (round 1: measures 1, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 17;
  round 2: measures 2 and 5) — never by amount. Amounts follow the
  published form: the FY2025 policy evaluation (FY2024 business)
  printed each measure's settlement total (the sum of its component
  promotion projects) on the measure evaluation sheet, so the
  FY2024 settlement is registered; the FY2026 evaluation (FY2025
  business, published 2026-09-30) no longer prints a per-measure
  settlement total — only the main projects' amounts per basic
  direction — so FY2025 carries no amount (main-project amounts are
  never summed into a measure total). The responsible department is
  the lead office named on the FY2025 evaluation sheet, the last
  edition that printed it. The official evaluation is the FY2026
  evaluation's final measure rating; for measures 12 and 17 the
  policy-evaluation committee returned a "要検討" (needs review)
  draft judgement, and the prefecture's review left the final
  rating unchanged, which the records state. All 85 target
  indicators of the ten measures are registered from the midterm
  implementation plan's indicator list (initial values and FY2027
  midterm targets); the FY2025 achievement rates and grades (A-D)
  published in the FY2026 evaluation's results table are recorded
  in each indicator's definition, with the grade converted to the
  registration assessment scale. FY2025 actual values appear in the
  FY2026 evaluation only as graphs, with no numeric table, so no
  FY2025 actual is taken from that book; where the midterm plan
  (February 2026 revision) prints an FY2025 value numerically —
  fifteen education indicators of measure 8 — that value is
  registered, and for the remaining indicators the latest actual
  printed in the plan's indicator list is registered.
