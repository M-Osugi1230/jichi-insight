# Hiroshima Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Hiroshima cluster stage 1 (Issue #915) — entity identity,
the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, and the
governor's current term. Unlike Shizuoka and Miyagi, Hiroshima
Prefecture has no Phase 9 registration (the Phase 9 file set has
no `34.json`), so this package stands on the prefecture's and the
MIC's own published documents alone.

- Municipality: 広島県 (`jp-local-340006`, official code 340006).
  The prefecture's corporate number 7000020340006 (stated in the
  footer of the official prefecture site) ends in the code, and
  the MIC settlement cards carry the Hiroshima sheet under
  code 34.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 1,151,390,000 thousand
  yen and prefectural tax revenue 349,242,060 thousand yen, from
  Bill No. 1 of the February 2026 regular session
  (県第1号議案「令和8年度広島県一般会計予算」), passed on
  2026-03-17 with the result stated verbatim as 「可決」 (the
  assembly's results table uses the 「可決／同意」 forms, not
  「原案可決」; only the Japanese Communist Party group voted
  against). The thousand-yen figures are the printed values in
  the budget explanation document (「令和8年広島県議会2月定例会
  予算説明書」), whose summary table of the general-account
  revenue and expenditure budget details states both amounts;
  the prefecture's publicity materials round the total to
  1兆1,514億円, which is not used.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between two primary sources — the MIC settlement
  cards (都道府県決算カード, Hiroshima sheet) and the fiscal
  situation collections (財政状況資料集) as hosted on the MIC
  site. All values agreed on every indicator and every breakdown
  category in all three years; nothing was guessed or recomputed.
  Arithmetic checks (revenue − expenditure = formal balance;
  formal balance − carryover resources = real balance;
  purpose/nature breakdown sums = total expenditure; the fund
  categories sum to the fund balance) all pass. Ratios are
  registered as published, never recomputed; the labelled
  current-balance ratio is used rather than the parenthesised
  variant (94.9 for FY2024 against the registered 94.0).
  Categories shown as "－" in the sources are omitted from the
  breakdowns (no value is guessed or zero-filled): 消防費・
  諸支出金・前年度繰上充用金・特別区財政調整交付金 in every
  year and 自動車取得税交付金 in FY2024 in the purpose breakdown,
  and 前年度繰上充用金・失業対策事業費 in the nature breakdown
  in every year. The debt breakdown's temporary fiscal measures
  bonds figure is derived by subtracting the collection's
  printed "excluding temporary fiscal measures bonds" balance
  from the total, and is labelled as derived in the record.
  The prefecture's own settlement summaries are general-account
  documents and were not used as a cross-check system (the
  FY2024 general-account revenue of 1,130,993,099 thousand yen
  differs from the ordinary-account total and is not mixed in).
- Plan: the top plan is the Hiroshima Vision
  (「安心▷誇り▷挑戦 ひろしまビジョン」), adopted in October
  2020 and revised in June 2026. Its basic philosophy —
  「将来にわたって、「広島に生まれ、育ち、住み、働いて
  良かった」と心から思える広島県の実現」 — is unchanged by the
  revision; the revised aim reads 「県民一人一人が「安心」の
  土台と「誇り」により、夢や希望に「挑戦」しています
  ～仕事も暮らしも。里もまちも。それぞれが望むライフスタイル
  の実現～」. The revision sets the direction for the next five
  years (「シン・ファミリーフレンドリー」) and five priority
  items, and the implementation layer is the revised action
  plan (FY2026-FY2030), which organises 17 policy areas under
  five policy pillars, each area carrying target values and
  KPIs, with the concrete project groups called "work"
  (ワーク). Progress is reported annually in the
  implementation report (主要施策の成果に関する説明書), which
  tracks each work package against its outcome targets.
- Governor: 横田 美香, current term 2025-11-29 to 2029-11-28.
  She won the term-expiry election of 2025-11-09 — all three
  candidates were newcomers running as independents — with
  552,614 votes (83.42% of valid votes, turnout 30.09%),
  checked directly against the prefectural election
  commission's official ballot-count result. Because this was
  a term-expiry election, the term starts the day after the
  previous term expired (2025-11-28, stated in the previous
  edition of the commission's term list) under the proviso of
  Article 259 of the Public Offices Election Act; no official
  document directly printing the 2025-11-29 inauguration date
  was located, which the evidence packet records honestly.
  The expiry date 2029-11-28 is stated verbatim in the
  prefectural election commission's official term list
  (「広島県知事 11.11.28」, as of 2026-07-07), so the statutory
  construction and the commission's own statement agree.
  Earlier terms are not registered.
