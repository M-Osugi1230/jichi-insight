# Akita Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Akita Prefecture's FY2026 general-account initial budget (total, with
the prefectural tax carried as an explicitly unpublished figure), the
FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements in the detailed 51-record form,
the current governor term, and a plan review of the prefecture's
comprehensive plan. Priority projects, promises and the assembly layer are
registered in later stages of the Akita cluster (Issue #1001).

- Municipality: Akita Prefecture (団体コード 050008, confirmed at the stage-0
  inventory from the e-Gov municipality-code list and prefectural official
  documents). The MIC settlement workbooks carry the prefecture's sheet
  under code 05 (sheet name 「6秋田県」).
- FY2026 budget: the assembly bill text (議案書) is not published on the
  prefecture's website. The general-account total of 604,145,000 thousand
  yen was fixed at the stage-0 inventory from the Finance Division's
  「財政状況（令和8年6月）」 revenue table (604,145 million yen) and the
  assembly's session summary (6,041億4,500万円). Bill No. 45 was decided
  「原案可決」 on 2026-03-19 by standing vote (39 to 1). The prefectural tax
  is published only in million yen (99,270 million yen, 16.4% of revenue);
  no web-published prefectural document carries the thousand-yen figure,
  so the local_tax record is registered with value_status "not_published"
  and no amount, rather than rounding the million-yen figure into a false
  exact value. The fixing route (Bill No. 45's first table, or the
  "補正前の額" chain of a supplementary budget bill) is recorded as an open
  question in the evidence packet.
- Settlements: the MIC settlement card (Akita Prefecture sheet) and the
  prefecture's fiscal-situation collection agree on all registered values
  except the two cells the card leaves to the collection (the carryover
  resources and the debt balance excluding temporary fiscal measures
  bonds, which the card does not print); the balance identities, the
  purpose/nature breakdown sums, the fund components and the debt
  components were all re-computed and agree. The current balance ratio
  uses the labelled value (89.5% for FY2024); the parenthesised variant
  excluding the special revenue-shortfall bonds and temporary fiscal
  measures bonds (89.7%) is not registered. The debt breakdown's
  temporary-fiscal-measures-bonds figure is derived by subtraction and is
  marked as such.
- Governor: Suzuki Kenta, first term, 2025-04-20 to 2029-04-19. The
  expiry date is printed in the prefectural election commission's
  official term-expiry list (令和11年4月19日, updated 2026-06-30); the
  start date is corroborated by the governor's own inauguration address
  (2025-04-21, stating he took office "yesterday") and follows the
  Article 259 proviso chain from the previous term's expiry
  (2025-04-19). The 2025-04-06 election result (273,270 votes) was read
  from the MIC election results report. The 2025 election bulletin is no
  longer carried on the election commission's current pages, but a copy
  hosted on the Yurihonjo City official site was verified byte-identical
  to its Wayback Machine snapshot of 2025-05-15 (SHA-256
  680355120c3afb5c17ece4fb9dd63ef086fdd8063953c6630f367a9d2f7d34f8), so
  the bulletin is registered as a manifesto source; the promise records
  themselves are created in the promise stage.
