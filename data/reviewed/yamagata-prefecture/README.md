# Yamagata Prefecture reviewed data

Stage 1 of cluster 17 registers Yamagata Prefecture (`jp-local-060003`) with
reviewed data:

- Municipality identity and the FY2026 initial budget: the general account
  total is JPY 700,284,000,000 and prefectural tax is JPY 115,300,000,000,
  from Bill No. 29 (FY2026 Yamagata Prefecture general account budget),
  passed ("可決", not "原案可決") at the February 2026 regular session on
  2026-03-16. The thousand-yen figures were confirmed via Table 1 ("amount
  before correction") of Supplementary Budget No. 1 (decided by the governor
  on 2026-05-27) and cross-checked against the budget overview figures in
  millions of yen.
- FY2022-FY2024 settlement detail: 17 ordinary-account indicators for three
  fiscal years. The Ministry of Internal Affairs settlement card and the
  prefecture-published fiscal situation collection agree on every value, and
  the accounting identities were verified (revenue minus expenditure equals
  the formal balance; the purpose and nature breakdowns sum to total
  expenditure; the three fund categories sum to the fund balance).
- In the debt breakdown, the balance excluding temporary fiscal measures
  bonds is the printed value in the collection's summary table; the
  temporary fiscal measures bonds figure is derived by subtraction and is
  marked as such in the breakdown note.
- Governor Yoshimura Mieko's current term is registered in the shared
  executive registry (elected on 2025-01-26 with 318,364 votes; term from
  2025-02-14 to 2029-02-13). The term start follows the previous term's
  expiry under the proviso of Article 259 of the Public Offices Election Act
  (open question OQ-1 in the stage-0 inventory). The prefecture's own
  term-expiry list PDF recorded at stage 0 no longer responds, so the live
  official statement used for the expiry date is the federation of
  prefectural election administration commissions' governor list, which
  states the same 2029-02-13 expiry.
- The 2025 election bulletin is reproduced in full in part 6 (pp. 104-105)
  of the Election Administration Commission's official record of the
  election and is downloadable from the prefecture's website, so its source
  is registered now; transcription and linking to the term's manifesto
  sources is stage 3 work.
- The plan review covers the Fourth Yamagata Prefecture Comprehensive
  Development Plan (adopted March 2020): a long-term concept plus an
  implementation plan whose official progress evaluation is published per
  policy, making "policy" (28 items) the stage 2 registration-unit
  candidate.
