# Kyoto Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Kyoto cluster stage 1 (Issue #932) — entity identity,
the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, and the
governor's current term. Kyoto Prefecture had no earlier
reviewed registration in this repository (the Kyoto City
package comes from the Phase 13/15 line), so this package
stands on the prefecture's and the MIC's own published
documents alone.

- Municipality: 京都府 (`jp-local-260002`, official code 260002).
  The prefecture's corporate number 2000020260002 (stated in
  the footer of the official prefecture site) ends in the
  code, and the MIC settlement cards carry the Kyoto sheet
  under code 26.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 1,043,260,000
  thousand yen and prefectural tax revenue 314,000,000
  thousand yen, from Bill No. 1 of the February 2026 regular
  session (第1号議案「令和8年度京都府一般会計予算」), passed on
  2026-03-12 with the result stated verbatim as 「原案可決」
  (the Japanese Communist Party group did not vote in favour).
  Both thousand-yen figures are the printed values in the
  bill itself (Article 1 and Table 1); the tax total also
  equals the sum of its twelve itemised components. The
  budget was compiled as a framework (骨格) budget ahead of
  the April 2026 governor election, and the same pre-revision
  total appears in the June 2026 supplementary bill's
  Table 1. The prefecture's publicity materials round the
  total to the hundred-million-yen level, which is not used.
  The stage-0 inventory could only confirm the tax figure to
  the million-yen level (314,000 million yen in the budget
  outline); the bill's Table 1 resolves it to the exact
  thousand-yen value.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between two primary sources — the MIC
  settlement cards (都道府県決算カード, Kyoto sheet) and the
  fiscal situation collections (財政状況資料集) as hosted on
  the MIC site. All values agreed on every indicator and
  every breakdown category in all three years; nothing was
  guessed or recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue −
  expenditure = formal balance; formal balance − carryover
  resources = real balance; purpose/nature breakdown sums =
  total expenditure; the fund categories sum to the fund
  balance) all pass. Ratios are registered as published,
  never recomputed; the labelled current-balance ratio is
  used rather than the parenthesised variant (98.5 / 95.7 /
  94.2). The debt-reduction fund （減債基金） is printed as
  "-" (no balance) in both sources in all three years, so
  the fund balance consists of the fiscal adjustment fund
  and the other special-purpose funds only; the printed
  "地方債現在高（臨時財政対策債除き）" of the collections is
  registered as a breakdown item and the temporary fiscal
  measures bond balance is the derived difference, marked as
  such in the breakdown note.
- Governor: 西脇 隆俊, third term, 2026-04-16 to 2030-04-15.
  He won the 2026-04-05 term-expiry election with 412,583
  votes (prefectural total in the election commission's own
  ballot-count results). The expiry date is stated directly
  in the election commission's official term list (as of
  2026-04-30); the start follows the Article 259 proviso
  construction and agrees with the officially published
  inauguration press conference and first-attendance date
  (2026-04-16). The election proclamation date, left
  media-only at stage 0, was confirmed directly in the
  prefectural gazette: Election Commission Notice No. 47 in
  Extra No. 13 (2026-03-19) sets the election date.
