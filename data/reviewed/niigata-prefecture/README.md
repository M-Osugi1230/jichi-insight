# Niigata Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Niigata Prefecture's FY2026 general-account initial budget (total and
prefectural tax), the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements in the
detailed 51-record form, the current governor term, and a plan review of the
prefecture's comprehensive plan. Priority projects, promises and the assembly
layer are registered in later stages of the Niigata cluster (Issue #945).

- Municipality: Niigata Prefecture (団体コード 150002; the prefecture's
  corporate number 5000020150002 ends in the municipality code, confirmed at
  the stage-0 inventory from the footer of the prefectural site). The MIC
  settlement workbooks carry the prefecture's sheet under code 15.
- FY2026 budget: the assembly bill text (議案書) is not published on the
  prefecture's website, so the thousand-yen figures were fixed from the
  Finance Division's official figures document (令和8年度当初予算計数資料),
  whose revenue and expenditure tables by 款 were cross-footed against the
  printed total of 1,169,750,000 thousand yen (prefectural tax 292,805,000
  thousand yen). Bill No. 1 was decided 「可決」 on 2026-03-27 (not
  「原案可決」; the only dissent recorded in the assembly's group table is
  Councillor Baba Hideyuki). Because refinancing bonds moved to the
  prefectural-bond management special account from FY2026, the total is not
  directly comparable with the FY2025 initial budget.
- Settlements: the MIC settlement card (Niigata Prefecture sheet) and the
  prefecture's fiscal-situation collection agree on all 51 registered values;
  the balance identities, the purpose/nature breakdown sums, the fund
  components and the debt components were all re-computed and agree. The fund
  balance has three components, including a debt-reduction fund with a
  positive balance (44,707,183 thousand yen at FY2024). The current balance
  ratio uses the labelled value (93.8% for FY2024); the parenthesised variant
  excluding the special revenue-shortfall bonds and temporary fiscal measures
  bonds (94.2%) is not registered. The debt breakdown's
  temporary-fiscal-measures-bonds figure is derived by subtraction and is
  marked as such.
- Governor: Hanazumi Hideyo, third term, 2026-06-10 to 2030-06-09. The expiry
  date is printed in the prefectural election commission's official
  term-expiry list (R12年6月9日, updated 2026-10-16); the start follows the
  Article 259 proviso chain from the previous term's expiry and matches the
  governor profile updated on the start date. The 2026-05-31 election result
  (554,012 votes) was read from the commission's own results page. The
  election bulletin for the 2026 election could not be located on the
  commission's current pages, the election special site, or the Wayback
  Machine, so manifesto_source_ids is empty and the question is carried as an
  open question for the promise stage.
