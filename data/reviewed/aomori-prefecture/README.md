# Aomori Prefecture reviewed package

Scope: Aomori cluster stage 1 (Issue #1072) — entity identity,
the current plan review, the FY2026 general-account initial
budget, the FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account settlements, and the
governor's current term. Aomori Prefecture had no earlier
reviewed deep-dive registration in this repository (no reviewed
package, no fiscal or settlement records; the Phase 9 plan
review record for the same basic plan exists separately), so
this package is a new registration standing on the prefecture's
and the MIC's own published documents alone; nothing was
replaced.

- Municipality: 青森県 (`jp-local-020001`, official code 020001).
  The prefecture's corporate number 2000020020001 (National Tax
  Agency corporate-number publication site) ends in the code.
- FY2026 initial budget: general account 751,400,000 thousand
  yen and prefectural tax revenue 162,183,592 thousand yen,
  from Bill No. 1 of the February 2026 regular session
  (議案第1号「令和8年度青森県一般会計予算」), passed on
  2026-03-24 with the result stated verbatim as 「原案可決」.
  The figures are the printed values in the finance
  division's category summary table （款別総括表）; the total
  also equals the pre-revision amount in Table 1 of the
  FY2026 general-account supplementary budget (No. 1) as
  promulgated in the prefectural gazette extra issue No. 71,
  and the tax figure is also printed in yen in the budget
  summary （当初予算案の概要）.
- Settlements: FY2022-FY2024 ordinary-account records were
  cross-checked between the MIC settlement cards
  （都道府県決算カード, Aomori sheet of the all-prefecture
  workbook) and the fiscal situation collections
  （財政状況資料集） published by the prefecture itself on its
  official site. All values agreed on every indicator and
  every breakdown category in all three years; nothing was
  guessed or recomputed. Arithmetic checks (revenue −
  expenditure = formal balance; formal balance − carryover
  resources = real balance; purpose/nature breakdown sums =
  total expenditure; the fund categories sum to the fund
  balance) all pass. Ratios are registered as published, never
  recomputed; the labelled current-balance ratio is used
  rather than the parenthesised variant. Cross-check design
  limit: both systems follow the national settlement-statistics
  formats and rest on the same underlying statistics, so the
  cross-check detects transcription and aggregation errors but
  is not an independent verification of the statistics
  themselves; the prefecture's statutory settlement documents
  （決算書・決算の概要） are on a general/special-account basis
  and cannot independently reproduce the ordinary-account
  aggregates. This limit is recorded in every settlement
  evidence packet. The printed "地方債現在高（臨時財政対策債
  を除く）" of the collections is registered as a breakdown
  item and the temporary fiscal measures bond balance is the
  derived difference, marked as such in the breakdown note.
- Governor: 宮下 宗一郎, first term, 2023-06-29 to 2027-06-28.
  He won the 2023-06-04 term-expiry election with 404,358
  votes — the figure printed in the prefectural election
  commission's official 『選挙の記録』; the four candidates'
  votes sum to the valid-vote total of 593,798, and turnout
  was 57.05%. No press figure is used. The start date is the
  inauguration day stated on the prefecture's official
  inauguration press-conference page and agrees with the day
  after the previous term's expiry (2023-06-28). The end date
  2027-06-28 is stated directly in the governors list of the
  Federation of Prefectural Election Administration
  Commissions; no document of the prefectural commission
  itself printing the end date has been located yet, and this
  is kept as an open question in the evidence packet. The
  2023 election bulletin survives on the election
  commission's current official pages and is registered as a
  manifesto source; promise extraction is deferred to stage 3.
- Review delegation: the review and promotion judgements in
  this package were made by Muse under the repository
  owner's delegation of 2026-11-15; they are not recorded as
  the owner's own review.
