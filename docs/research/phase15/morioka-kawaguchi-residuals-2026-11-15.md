Primary-source follow-ups for two open items in the Jichi Insight Phase 15 reviewed packages.
Researched 2026-11-15 via web search and page fetches of official sources.

## ITEM 1 — Morioka City （盛岡市）: FY2026 budget bill No. 3 vote result

### Conclusion
**No HTML vote-result page could be located.** After seven searches with varied phrasing
（議決結果 / 審議結果 / 会議結果 / 定例会の結果 / 会議録検索）, every official result for
Morioka's 議決結果 points to the council newsletter PDF （もりおか市議会だより） on
city.morioka.iwate.jp. The newsletter itself states that bill *summaries* are on the council
homepage (「議案の概要については、市議会ホームページで確認することができます」), while the
vote *results* are published in the newsletter. Morioka appears to publish 議決結果 only in
PDF (the newsletter's 「提出議案と議決結果」 page, plus per-session 賛否一覧表 PDFs).

### Closest official page (use as the stable locator)
- **もりおか市議会だより No.241 （令和8年5月1日発行）, p.8 「提出議案と議決結果」**
- URL: https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/056/302/241.8.pdf
- Entry for the bill (quoted from the fetched page):
  > 「第３号 令和８年度盛岡市一般会計予算 可決 （多数）」
- Per-member vote table on the same page (quoted):
  > 「議案第３号 … 可決」
- Page footer (quoted): 「（8） 2026. 5. 1 もりおか市議会だより」「編集・発行 盛岡市議会議会広報委員会」

### Session / date context
- Session: 令和8年3月定例会 (March 2026 regular session), convened 2026-02-19;
  final plenary and vote on **2026-03-26**.
  - The schedule printed in 市議会だより No.240 lists 「本会議（最終日）3月26日」 for the March session.
  - NHK: 「盛岡市議会の3月定例会は26日、最終日を迎え、一般会計の総額で1200億円余りに上る新年度＝令和8年度の当初予算などが可決・成立して閉会しました」
    (https://news.web.nhk/newsweb/na/nb-6040029374)
  - FNN: 2026年度一般会計予算 (¥126,510,000 thousand) passed by majority on 2026-03-26
    (https://www.fnn.jp/articles/-/1021150)

### Note for the reviewed package
The open residual ("direct confirmation on the official vote-result HTML page") cannot be
closed as originally framed, because no such HTML page was found. Recommendation: change
the locator to the newsletter PDF above (official council publication, 市議会だより No.241,
p.8) and reword the residual to state that Morioka publishes vote results in the newsletter
PDF rather than an HTML page.

## ITEM 2 — Kawaguchi City （川口市）: FY2024 settlement certification metadata

### Answer
- **Bill number: 議案第183号**
- **Title: 令和6年度川口市一般会計及び各種特別会計決算認定について**
  (covers the general account and all special accounts, including the known general-account
  values: revenue ¥259,800,531,831 / expenditure ¥247,943,564,747)
- **Submitted: 令和7年9月3日 (2025-09-03)**, at the Reiwa 7 3rd (September) regular session
- **September outcome: 継続審査 (continued deliberation)**, 2025-09-30, 議決番号 189
- **Certification date: 令和7年11月28日 (2025-11-28)** — opening day of the
  Reiwa 7 4th (December) regular session
- **Result: 認定 (certified), by majority vote （賛成多数）**

### Caveat
The December session's own 議決結果表 PDF on kawaguchi.gsl-service.net (the R7.12
counterpart of the R7.9 table in source 2) could not be located by search, so the
December 議決番号 for bill 183 is **not confirmed**. The certification date (2025-11-28)
rests on the chain: the December session opened 2025-11-28 with the certification vote
scheduled for the opening day (JCP Kawaguchi newsletter No.1814, 2025-11-23), the
mayor's same-day official address thanking the council for the certification just granted,
the councilor's same-day report of the vote, and the official 告示 of 2025-12-01
publishing the settlement as decided at that session.
